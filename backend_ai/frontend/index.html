Essay Question

Climate change is one of the most debated topics in modern society. Write a persuasive essay arguing either for or against the urgency of immediate government action to address climate change. Your essay should include:

  • A clear position statement (thesis)
  • At least three supporting arguments with evidence
  • Recognition and refutation of opposing viewpoints
  • A compelling conclusion with a call to action
